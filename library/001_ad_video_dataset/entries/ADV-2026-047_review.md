## Summary

A mock-epic comedic brand film built as a "family motto (가훈)" heritage saga and a high-art parody. A lineage of goggled "ancestors" is immortalised in oil-painted portraits (parodying a Vermeer pearl-earring portrait, Manet's "Luncheon on the Grass", and the "Lunch atop a Skyscraper" photograph with selective colour). A deadpan carved stop-motion puppet protagonist — teal delivery helmet, round goggle-spectacles — is painted into existence, surveys a whole city of food through binoculars at dusk, summons a staccato roll-call of macro food beauty shots with bold caption cards, plants a flag atop a giant burger like a mountaineer, and presides over a banquet payoff punning on the brand name (민족). Resolves on the app end-card "배달의민족 2.0". Two alternating layout systems: reverent centred framed artwork on dark ornate walls (bottom-centre narration subtitles) and hard-backlit macro food on near-black grounds (bold white caption upper-left). Warm museum-amber grade, crushed blacks, teal + food-red accents, fine filmic grain.
