## Inferred Creative Thinking

- **Insight**: Most people feel anxious about money because financial knowledge was never systematically taught — they encounter each financial decision as a novel, isolating problem.
- **Persona**: Korean urban professional 20s–30s earning first steady income; has Toss app but doesn't know how to optimize financial life; educated but financially illiterate.
- **Moment**: When someone faces a concrete financial decision (buying a car, signing a lease, opening a savings account) and realizes they don't know what to do.
- **Punchline**: Toss already has your money; now they're giving you the knowledge to understand what to do with it.
- **Brand fit**: Toss extends mission from 'making finance easy to use' to 'making finance easy to understand'.
